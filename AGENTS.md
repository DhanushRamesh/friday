# Instructions

Read [`DEVELOPMENT.md`](DEVELOPMENT.md) before making any change to this repository, and follow it.

It records decisions the owner has already made, conventions this codebase
follows, and how the owner wants work carried out. Three points matter most:

1. **Do one piece of work, then stop and wait for approval** before starting
   the next. Do not build ahead.
2. **Never add `Co-Authored-By` or generated-by attribution** to commits or
   pull requests.
3. The assistant is called **FRIDAY**, never JARVIS.

When the owner states a preference or makes a decision, record it in
`DEVELOPMENT.md` in the same turn.
